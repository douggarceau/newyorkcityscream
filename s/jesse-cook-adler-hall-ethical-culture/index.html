Jesse Cook Plays Adler Hall at the New York Society for Ethical Culture

Friday, November 13 at 7:30 PM in Adler Hall, New York Society for Ethical Culture, 2 West 64th Street.

Adler Hall sits quietly on the Upper West Side, and many New Yorkers walk past it without knowing what happens inside. On Friday, November 13 at 7:30 PM, the hall hosts a concert by Jesse Cook, a musician whose listings describe him as a New Age performer with a warm, melodic sound.

The Society and its hall

The New York Society for Ethical Culture was founded in 1876 by Felix Adler, who set out to build a community around ethical living rather than a single creed. Its meeting house has long hosted lectures, recitals and community programs, so an evening there feels more like a neighborhood gathering than a large arena show.

The music

The concert page does not list a program or a set order, so arrive open to a single-artist evening in a small hall. Concerts of this kind are a good way to hear a guitarist up close, with the room carrying every detail of the playing.

Tickets and getting there

When this was checked, tickets were not yet on sale, and the listing invites visitors to sign up for an alert when they are. Check the ticket listing and the Society's own calendar for updates. Adler Hall is at 2 West 64th Street, a few blocks from the 66th Street stop on the 1 train.
