Pianist Cristiana Pegoraro Plays Weill Recital Hall on November 12

Thursday, November 12 at 8 PM in Weill Recital Hall at Carnegie Hall, presented by LKI Artists International.

A solo piano recital is one of the most intimate evenings in classical music, and Cristiana Pegoraro brings one to Weill Recital Hall on Thursday, November 12 at 8 PM. Weill is the smallest of Carnegie Hall's three performance rooms, which means the audience sits close to the instrument.

The hall

Carnegie Hall's three rooms share one address but have very different characters. Stern Auditorium is the largest, Zankel Hall is mid-sized and Weill Recital Hall is the most intimate of the three. A recital in Weill is a good first visit for anyone who wants to hear serious piano playing without the scale of a full orchestra.

Who is presenting

LKI Artists International presents the recital, as listed on the Carnegie Hall calendar. The calendar names Pegoraro as the performer but does not give the program, so ticket buyers should check the Carnegie Hall website for the repertoire and any update before the evening.

Getting there and tickets

Weill Recital Hall is in the Carnegie Hall complex at 881 Seventh Avenue, at West 57th Street, a short walk from the Columbus Circle subway station. Tickets are available through Carnegie Hall's box office and website.
