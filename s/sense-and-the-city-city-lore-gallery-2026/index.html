Sense & the City Invites Visitors to Taste, Touch and Hear New York

At City Lore Gallery in the East Village, the show runs on weekends from noon to 6 p.m. through January 18, 2027, with admission at no charge.

Sense & the City: Exploring New York City Through the Five Senses turns a gallery visit into something closer to a walk through the five boroughs. The exhibition opened on October 8 at City Lore Gallery, 56 East 1st Street in the East Village, and runs through January 18, 2027.

Hours and admission

The gallery is open Saturdays and Sundays from noon to 6 p.m. Admission is at no charge, and the listing mentions no advance sign-up. The nearest subway is the F train to Second Avenue.

An archive of New York places

The show is curated by Caitlin Van Dusen, who writes the City Lore blog. It presents close to 200 places she has covered over 18 years of writing, drawn from all five boroughs, with each one shown through a photograph and a short excerpt from her blog.

Five senses, one city

Visitors can smell the city through scratch-and-sniff strips, from the balsam at sidewalk Christmas tree stands to the brine of a Bronx pickle factory. A tactile rail holds objects that include dirt from Aqueduct Racetrack and a basket woven from invasive vines found on Staten Island. For sound, a guest residency by Surround Sounds Piano_QNS brings in an upright piano that artists Arthur Fleischmann and Daniel Giachetti retrofitted so that each key plays a sound recorded in Queens.

Why go

The exhibition has a warm, neighborhood feel and suits anyone who likes the idea of New York as a collection of small, specific places. It is a good match for a weekend afternoon with children or friends, and it rewards a slow visit.
