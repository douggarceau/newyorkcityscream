Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone in Concert With the New York Philharmonic

Sunday, December 20 at 2 PM at David Geffen Hall, Lincoln Center, with the orchestra performing the John Williams score live.

The New York Philharmonic presents Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone in Concert on Sunday, December 20 at 2 PM in David Geffen Hall at Lincoln Center, 132 West 65th Street. The orchestra performs John Williams's score live as the 2001 film plays.

Williams's music for the first Harry Potter film is among the most recognizable scores of the last quarter century. Hearing a full symphony orchestra play it in the hall where the Philharmonic makes its home gives the familiar themes a new weight, and the program suits families spending the holidays in the city.

Getting seats

Seats are sold through the New York Philharmonic's ticketing pages and the usual resale sites. Check the Philharmonic's listing for current availability and any age guidance before you go.
