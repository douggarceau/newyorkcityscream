Laughs on Broadway: Comedy Festival Nights and Oliver and Meyers at the Beacon

The Beacon Theatre hosts New York Comedy Festival shows Nov. 12-14 and John Oliver with Seth Meyers on Nov. 22.

The Beacon Theatre, at 2124 Broadway at 74th Street turns into a comedy house for much of November.

The New York Comedy Festival presents three straight nights there: Jordan Jensen on Thursday, Nov. 12, Ilana Glazer Live! on Friday, Nov. 13, and Daniel Sloss with his show Bitter on Saturday, Nov. 14.

Then on Sunday, Nov. 22, at 7:30 p.m., John Oliver and Seth Meyers share the stage. A second Oliver and Meyers show is set for Dec. 13. Tickets are sold through Ticketmaster, with all-in pricing that includes fees.

Music fans get their turn too: Michelle Branch plays the Beacon on Thursday, Nov. 5.

Check beacontheatre.com for venue rules and showtimes before you go.
