The Senior Concert Orchestra of New York Plays Carnegie Hall Sunday

Sunday, November 8 at 1 PM in Stern Auditorium at Carnegie Hall, 881 Seventh Avenue.

On Sunday afternoon, the Senior Concert Orchestra of New York takes the stage in Stern Auditorium at Carnegie Hall. The concert begins at 1 PM, which makes it an easy afternoon out for anyone who loves orchestral music and wants to hear a community orchestra in one of the most famous rooms in the world.

A long relationship with Carnegie

Carnegie Hall's own archive lists the Senior Concert Orchestra of New York among its performing groups in seasons going back to 1972 and 1973, and the group has returned in later decades. Local 802, the New York musicians' union, has also published a photo essay of the orchestra at a dress rehearsal in Carnegie Hall.

What to expect

The Carnegie calendar does not list a program or a conductor for this concert, so the best way to learn what will be played is to check the Carnegie Hall website before you go. Stern Auditorium is the largest of the hall's three performance rooms, and it is the room where the group performs on this date.

Getting there and tickets

Carnegie Hall is at 881 Seventh Avenue at West 57th Street. The N, Q, R and W trains stop at 57th Street, and the 1 and A, B, C and D trains serve nearby Columbus Circle. Tickets and seating are handled through Carnegie Hall's box office and website.
