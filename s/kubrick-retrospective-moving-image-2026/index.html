Kubrick: A Full Retrospective Screens in Astoria Through Dec. 27

The Museum of the Moving Image's Stanley Kubrick retrospective runs at the Redstone Theater in Astoria through December 27, timed to a new Criterion box set.

Stanley Kubrick made some of the most talked-about films of the last century, and this season the Museum of the Moving Image in Astoria is giving his work a full run. The series, Kubrick: A Retrospective, is timed to the Criterion Collection's new box set, The Complete Kubrick. It opened on November 1 and continues through December 27 at the museum's Redstone Theater.

What is on the screen

The retrospective covers the director's whole filmography, from the early Fear and Desire and Killer's Kiss to later films such as 2001: A Space Odyssey, Barry Lyndon and Eyes Wide Shut. The coming weeks offer a good run of films. Barry Lyndon plays Friday, November 27 at 3 p.m. and again on Saturday, November 28 at 5:30 p.m. Fear and Desire and Killer's Kiss share a Sunday, December 13 program, and 2001: A Space Odyssey screens Friday, December 11 at 6:30 p.m.

Eyes Wide Shut plays on December 18 and December 26, and Spartacus closes the run on Sunday, December 27 at 3:15 p.m.

Why it matters

Kubrick worked across genres, from war and science fiction to period drama, and he often reshaped the rules of each one. Time Out's preview describes his films as eccentric and narratively experimental, a phrase that fits a retrospective built to be watched in order. For anyone who has only seen one or two of these films, the complete run is a rare chance to follow how his camera style and storytelling changed over his career.

Tickets and the venue

According to the listing, adult tickets are $17.50, seniors and students pay $12, children pay $10 and museum members pay $7, with prices varying by eligibility. The Museum of the Moving Image is at 36-01 35th Avenue in Astoria, Queens.

Screenings run on weekday evenings and weekend afternoons, so a matinee fits easily into a weekend plan.
