Charles Lloyd Plays Two Nights at the Blue Note

Jazz great Charles Lloyd plays the Blue Note in Greenwich Village Nov. 18 and 19.

Jazz great Charles Lloyd plays the Blue Note Jazz Club on Wednesday, Nov. 18, and Thursday, Nov. 19, with multiple sets; the Thursday late set has doors at 10:30 p.m.

The Blue Note is at 131 W. Third Street in Greenwich Village.

Tickets are on sale through the club's ticketing partner. Check set times when you buy.
