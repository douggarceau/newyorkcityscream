Apollo's Fire and Soprano Jeanine De Bique Bring Baroque Music to Zankel Hall

Sunday, Nov. 15 at Zankel Hall in Carnegie Hall, Midtown, Jeannette Sorrell leads Apollo's Fire and soprano Jeanine De Bique; the start time is still to be confirmed.

On Sunday, Nov. 15, the baroque ensemble Apollo's Fire comes to Zankel Hall, the 599-seat room at 881 Seventh Avenue that sits beneath the main Stern Auditorium of Carnegie Hall. Jeannette Sorrell leads the group, and soprano Jeanine De Bique joins for an evening of Baroque music. It is the kind of small, close concert where the sound of a period ensemble fills the room.

The program

The listing names ten composers, among them Handel, Rameau, Lully, Marais, Purcell and Vivaldi, though it does not spell out individual works. The mix makes the evening a sampler of French, English, Italian and German repertoire, with the singing and the instruments taking turns in the spotlight.

Tickets

The listing used for this story takes reservations by email and books the best available seats, with adjacent seats booked together. Vouchers are mailed to print and exchange at the theater. The start time was not given in the sources used here, so check Carnegie Hall's own calendar for the time and any updates before you go.

The Zankel Hall setting

Zankel Hall seats far fewer people than the main auditorium above it, so every seat is close to the stage. That scale suits the chamber-sized sound of a period ensemble, where the balance between voices and instruments is easy to hear.

Apollo's Fire is known for period-style performances of Baroque music, and Jeannette Sorrell, the ensemble's founder and music director, leads the group on this program.
