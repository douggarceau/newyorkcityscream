Frank Huang Plays Mendelssohn With the New York Philharmonic

Thursday, November 19 at 7:30 PM at David Geffen Hall in Lincoln Center, with further concerts on November 20 and 21.

The New York Philharmonic opens a run of concerts at David Geffen Hall on Thursday, November 19, at 7:30 PM, with violinist Frank Huang as the featured soloist in a program of music by Felix Mendelssohn. The listings show further performances on November 20 and 21, so the orchestra's Lincoln Center week stretches across the weekend.

Mendelssohn with a soloist

Mendelssohn wrote much of his most admired music while still in his twenties, and his concertos are staples of the violin repertoire. Hearing a soloist play his writing with a full orchestra is one of the simplest pleasures in the classical calendar, and the Philharmonic's Lincoln Center setting makes the evening a good fit for anyone new to live orchestral music.

Getting there

David Geffen Hall sits at 132 West 65th Street on the Lincoln Center campus, a short walk from the 66th Street station on the 1 train. The campus also houses the Metropolitan Opera and the Juilliard School, so an evening there can easily pair with a stroll along the plaza.
