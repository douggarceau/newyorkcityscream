Harlem Gospel Choir Sings at the Blue Note on Sunday, November 22

Sunday, November 22 at 1:30 PM at the Blue Note jazz club in Greenwich Village.

The World Famous Harlem Gospel Choir brings its voices to the Blue Note jazz club at 131 West 3rd Street on Sunday, November 22, at 1:30 PM. The Sunday afternoon start makes it an easy outing for anyone who wants gospel music without a late night.

About the choir

The listing names the choir as its lineup and links to a separate page with its full schedule of dates, which means this Blue Note show is one stop on a longer run of performances.

Getting in

Tickets are sold through the event listing, with prices starting at $54 before fees. Arrive a little early for a good view of the stage.
