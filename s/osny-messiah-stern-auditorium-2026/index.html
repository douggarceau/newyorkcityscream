Handel's Messiah Returns to Carnegie Hall With the Oratorio Society

Monday, December 21 at 7 p.m. in Stern Auditorium at Carnegie Hall in Midtown, with soloists Jen Zetlan, Daniel Moody, Brian Giebler and Jonathan Woody.

The Oratorio Society of New York brings Handel's Messiah to Carnegie Hall on Monday, December 21, at 7 p.m. The performance takes place in Stern Auditorium / Perelman Stage. The society has performed the oratorio every year for 152 consecutive years, which makes it one of the longest-running holiday concert traditions in the city.

The performance

The program opens with a stirring orchestral introduction and includes the famous Hallelujah chorus, which the society presents as the essential musical event of its season. The soloists are soprano Jen Zetlan, countertenor Daniel Moody, tenor Brian Giebler and bass-baritone Jonathan Woody. The society's page does not name the conductor, so check the printed program at the hall.

Tickets and location

Single tickets are sold through Carnegie Hall's calendar page for the concert. Carnegie Hall is at 881 Seventh Avenue, at 57th Street, in Midtown. Ticket prices are not stated on the society's page.

Why go

Messiah is one of the great seasonal works for chorus, and hearing a full choir sing it in Carnegie's main hall is a traditional way to start the holiday season. For a December evening in Midtown, it is an easy choice.
