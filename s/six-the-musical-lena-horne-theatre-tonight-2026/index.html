Six the Musical Plays the Lena Horne Theatre Tonight at 7 PM

Sunday, November 8 at 7 PM in the Lena Horne Theatre at 256 West 47th Street in Midtown.

Six the Musical takes the stage at the Lena Horne Theatre tonight with a 7 PM curtain. The theatre stands at 256 West 47th Street in the Theater District of Midtown Manhattan, a short walk from Times Square, and it makes an easy evening plan for a Sunday in the city.

The show retells the lives of Henry VIII's six wives as a pop concert. Each queen steps forward in turn to tell her own story, backed by a band and a score that blends pop, dance and history. Its mix of sharp humor and big sing-along numbers has made it a favorite with families, younger theatergoers and longtime fans of musical theatre.

Plan the evening

Arrive a little before 7 PM to find your seat and collect any tickets at the box office. The Times Square subway complex is a short walk from the theatre, and the 1, 2, 3, 7, N, Q, R and W trains all stop there. Seats and prices vary by performance, so check the show's official Broadway listing or a licensed ticket seller before you leave home.
