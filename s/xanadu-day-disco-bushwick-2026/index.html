Xanadu Day Disco Brings All-Ages Roller Skating to Bushwick

The daytime session runs Saturday, November 21 from noon to 6 p.m. at Xanadu in Bushwick, Brooklyn, and is open to all ages.

Xanadu Day Disco brings all-ages roller skating to Bushwick on Saturday, November 21, from noon to 6 p.m. The afternoon session is a bright, daytime version of a disco night, open to skaters of every age.

A daytime option

The listing describes the session as all-ages, which makes the afternoon a good choice for families, first-timers and anyone who wants a relaxed way to spend a Saturday. Young skaters and adults can take part together.

Where and when

Xanadu is in Bushwick, Brooklyn. The session runs from noon to 6 p.m. on Saturday, November 21. Check the venue's listing for skate rental and entry details before you go.
