Dolly Parton's Broadway Musical Starts Previews Dec. 7

DOLLY: A True Original Musical moves into the St. James Theatre for previews, with an opening set for Jan. 19.

Dolly Parton's life story is heading to Broadway. DOLLY: A True Original Musical starts previews at the St. James Theatre on Dec. 7, with an opening night set for Jan. 19, 2027.

Parton wrote the music and co-wrote the book with Maria S. Schlatter. Bartlett Sher directs, Mandy Moore choreographs, Derek McLane designs the sets and Stephen Oremus is the music supervisor. Those are the names on the production page.

Katie Rose Clarke plays Dolly Parton, Carrie St. Louis plays Dolly and Norah Nunes plays Little Dolly. John Behlmann plays Carl Dean, Gabriel Ebert plays Porter Wagoner and Crystal Kellogg plays Judy Ogle. The cast also includes an ensemble.

Where and how to get in

The St. James is at 246 West 44th Street, in the Theater District. The production page links to ATG Tickets for individual seats, and groups of 10 or more can book through Broadway in Bound. The page we read did not list prices.

Previews run before the official opening, so the show can still change between now and Jan. 19. If you want to see it before the reviews are in, the first week of previews is the time to go. If you want the version critics write about, plan for the opening.

The production page names a full creative team and a cast with a lead who plays the artist herself, so the show is built around one performer's story from the first song. Expect the staging, the songs and the pacing to settle during previews before the January opening.
