HUM Book Their First Full Tour in 12 Years. Earplugs Recommended.

The Illinois band will hit Terminal 5, Roadrunner and Showbox SoDo in 2027, its first full run since 2015.

HUM is going back out for real. The Illinois band has booked a 12-date U.S. tour for 2027, its first full tour since 2015, according to TicketNews.

What got announced

The run starts March 3 at The Eastern in Atlanta and wraps July 17 at The Warfield in San Francisco, TicketNews reports. It comes in chunks: a Texas swing in early March, Denver and Kansas City in April, an East Coast leg in May and two West Coast shows in July.

The Life and Times open every night, per TicketNews. Pelican join the four East Coast shows in May, and Spotlights take the two West Coast dates.

The dates that matter here

New York gets Terminal 5 on May 22. Boston follows the next night, May 23, at Roadrunner. Seattle gets Showbox SoDo on July 13, according to the TicketNews listing.

The full itinerary: Atlanta (March 3), Houston's House of Blues (March 5), Dallas's Bomb Factory (March 6), Austin's United Heritage Hall (March 7), Denver's Mission Ballroom (April 22), Kansas City's Midland Theatre (April 24), Philadelphia's Franklin Music Hall (May 20), Washington's Anthem (May 21), then New York, Boston, Seattle and San Francisco.

Why this one is a big deal

HUM have been a slow-burn band for a long time. TicketNews notes that the band's Slide Away appearances earlier in 2026 were its first performances in nearly seven years, and those sets included the first live versions of songs from Inlet, the band's 2020 album.

For anyone who missed those sets, this is the wider shot at hearing the Inlet material live. The dates are spread across four months and both coasts, with big-room stops in three of the country's loudest music cities.

Tickets

The TicketNews report does not list an on-sale date or prices. It points fans to the band's site, h-u-m.net, for ticket details. No new album has been announced alongside the tour.
