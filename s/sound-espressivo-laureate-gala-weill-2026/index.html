Sound Espressivo Laureate Gala Brings Competition Winners to Weill Hall

The gala runs Monday, November 23 at 7 p.m. in Weill Recital Hall at Carnegie Hall, with two world premieres and winners from eight countries.

The Sound Espressivo Laureate Gala brings competition winners to the stage of Weill Recital Hall at Carnegie Hall on Monday, November 23 at 7 p.m. The concert is the gala for the winners of the Sound Espressivo competition, which runs through Virtual Concert Halls, a platform that hosts live-streamed juried competitions and festivals. Progressive Musicians is named as a presenter.

The performers

Soprano Stacey Stofferahn and the Swedish singer and composer Sophie Dunér, who has roots in jazz, are among the featured voices. Maurizio Mastrinì, an Italian pianist and crossover composer, will perform five of his own original works, including a US premiere. The New York Chamber Players, conducted by Giacomo Franci, take part in the program.

Two world premieres

The evening includes two new works. Lowe Finney's Longing, for voice and orchestra, is sung by Stofferahn, and Sophie Dunér's Hey Doctor, for voice and strings, is performed by Dunér herself.

The repertoire

The classical program moves from Bach and Haydn through Chopin, Liszt and Rachmaninoff to Debussy, Elgar, Puccini and Khachaturian, with Beethoven and Saint-Saëns also on the list. Entrants came from the United States, Canada, Sweden, Italy, China, France, Germany and Korea, and the winners were chosen from live online auditions held in early fall.
