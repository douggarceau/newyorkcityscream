Trinity Choir Pairs Marianna Martines With Mozart at Trinity Church

Wednesday, November 18 from 1 to 2 p.m. at Trinity Church, 89 Broadway, Lower Manhattan, in the Concerts at One series.

Lunchtime music does not get much better than this. Trinity Church's Concerts at One series brings a midday program on Wednesday, November 18, with Trinity Choir and the NOVUS orchestra under the baton of Melissa Attebury.

The program places the Vienna composer Marianna Martines beside works by Mozart. Martines worked in the same city and era as Mozart, yet she is little known to most audiences today, and this concert offers a generous introduction to her music.

The setting

Trinity Church stands at the head of Wall Street, and its concert series gives the lower Manhattan lunch hour a rare sense of calm. The hour-long performance is an easy stop for anyone working or visiting downtown.

Practical details

The concert runs from 1 to 2 p.m. Trinity's season announcement lists the program, and the church's events pages carry ticket and seating information before you go.
