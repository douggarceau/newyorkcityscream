The Chats Bring Half Cut to Seattle, Boston and Brooklyn

The Australian punks drop their third album in March, then play 30 North American club dates with Chain Whip and Gymshorts.

What happened

The Chats, the Australian punk band, are booked for a long run of North American club shows. TicketNews reported that the band will play 30 dates across the continent in spring 2027, after a third studio album, Half Cut, lands March 5, 2027, via Virgin Music Group.

Chain Whip and Gymshorts open every North American show, according to TicketNews.

The local dates

Seattle gets the early slot: April 10 at The Showbox.

Boston follows on April 27 at the Paradise Rock Club.

New York's show is in Brooklyn on April 29 at Warsaw.

There is no Rhode Island date on the list.

The rest of the run

The tour opens April 7 at the UC Theatre in Berkeley and works up the West Coast through Bend, Portland and Vancouver before cutting across Boise, Salt Lake City, Denver, Omaha, Kansas City, Minneapolis, Chicago and Detroit, then into Toronto and Montreal. After the Northeast it heads south through Baltimore and Atlanta, plays five Florida dates, swings through Austin, San Antonio, Santa Fe, Phoenix and San Diego, and closes May 18 at The Roxy in West Hollywood, per TicketNews.

A European and U.K. run follows in September and October 2027, ending with three nights at London's Electric Ballroom, TicketNews reported.

Tickets

The artist presale started November 4 and general sale opened November 6 through Ticketmaster, according to TicketNews. Details are at the band's site, thechats.com.

Why it is worth circling

The timing is the hook. The band will be touring an album that will be barely a month old, so these sets should lean on new material. And the routing keeps them in clubs and theaters, from the Paradise to the Showbox, rather than sheds or arenas. If you want punk at close range next spring, these are the nights.

The same TicketNews report also mentions a 17-date run at home in Australia in November and December.
