Broadway Magic Hour Brings a Family Magic Matinee on November 21

Saturday, November 21 from 2 to 3 p.m., with Jim Vines and Carl Mercurio, a one-hour family show currently listed as sold out.

Broadway Magic Hour is a one-hour family matinee on Saturday, November 21, from 2 to 3 p.m. It is built around magicians Jim Vines and Carl Mercurio, who mix magic, comedy, mind-reading and grand illusions into a show meant to work for a wide range of ages, from about 5 to 105 according to the producers' listing.

What happens

Kids are invited onstage to help with the tricks, and the audience joins in throughout. Because the show is short and built for families, it suits a weekend afternoon between other plans.

Getting in

The ticket listing is marked sold out, so check the ticket page for returns or later dates. The page gives the venue only as a Midtown ZIP code, 10019, so confirm the street address on your confirmation before you go. Ticket prices were not listed when the page was checked.

Why it stands out

A magic show with audience participation fills a different gap than the large Broadway musicals. For a parent looking for a Saturday afternoon outing in Midtown, it is a compact and lively choice.
