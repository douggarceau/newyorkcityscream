Wynton Marsalis Leads Swinging Cities at Jazz at Lincoln Center

Friday and Saturday, November 6 and 7 at 7:30 PM in the Rose Theater, Broadway at West 60th Street.

Wynton Marsalis and The Jazz at Lincoln Center Orchestra bring Marsalis' Swinging Cities to the Rose Theater for two evenings this weekend. The program is built around the cities that have shaped the orchestra's travels, and it reflects a career spent playing for audiences around the world.

The program

The concert gathers three of Marsalis' extended works: the Vitoria Suite, the Shanghai Suite and the Marciac Suite. Each one is a portrait of a place, written from the experience of touring and the friendships that grew out of those visits.

Tickets and timing

Both performances begin at 7:30 PM. The listing shows Friday, November 6 as sold out and Saturday, November 7 still available, and says that tickets are sold through the venue's box office with a full cancellation guarantee for canceled shows. Check availability before you plan the weekend.

Getting there

Frederick P. Rose Hall stands at Columbus Circle, at Broadway and West 60th Street, beside Central Park. The 1 train stops at 59th Street and Columbus Circle, and the A, B, C and D trains also serve the Columbus Circle station a short walk away.
