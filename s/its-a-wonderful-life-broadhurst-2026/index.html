It's a Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play Opens at the Broadhurst

Opening night is Wednesday, November 18 at the Broadhurst Theatre, with a limited holiday run through January 3, 2027.

It's a Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play opens tonight, Wednesday, November 18, at the Broadhurst Theatre in the Theater District. Previews began on November 4, and the production is set for a limited holiday run through January 3, 2027.

The show is staged as a live radio play, adapting the story of George Bailey, a man in the small town of Bedford Falls who learns how much his life has mattered to the people around him. The tale comes from the beloved 1946 film, and this version gives it the feel of a radio broadcast, with the cast performing the way a studio cast would.

The company

Andrew Rannells plays George Bailey, with Megan Hilty as Mary Bailey and Tituss Burgess as Clarence. Harry Hamlin and Lisa Rinna take on several roles, including Mr. Potter and Violet.

Getting tickets

Tickets are sold through Telecharge. The Broadhurst sits in the heart of the Theater District, a short walk from Times Square, which makes it an easy addition to a holiday night out.

Whether you know the film by heart or are discovering it for the first time, this production is a warm way to begin the holiday season on Broadway.
