Kevin Puts's Bassoon Concerto Gets Its World Premiere at Geffen Hall

Santtu-Matias Rouvali leads the New York Philharmonic at the Wu Tsai Theater on Nov. 25, 27 and 28 and Dec. 1 at 7:30 p.m., with bassoonist Judith LeClair.

The New York Philharmonic opens a four-concert run at David Geffen Hall with the world premiere of Kevin Puts's Bassoon Concerto. The work is a Philharmonic co-commission with the St. Louis Symphony Orchestra and the Minnesota Orchestra, and the solo part belongs to bassoonist Judith LeClair. Santtu-Matias Rouvali conducts the program.

When and where

The concerts take place in the Wu Tsai Theater at David Geffen Hall, 10 Lincoln Center Plaza, on the Upper West Side. Performances are on Wednesday, Nov. 25, Friday, Nov. 27 and Saturday, Nov. 28 at 7:30 p.m., and on Tuesday, Dec. 1 at 7:30 p.m. The hall sits on the Lincoln Center campus, so the plaza is the easiest place to start your evening.

The program

Florence Price's Dances in the Canebrakes, in the orchestration by William Grant Still, opens the evening. The Puts concerto follows, and Schubert's Symphony No. 9, the Great, closes the program. Pairing a new concerto with a large Schubert symphony gives a recent work the setting of a standard repertoire program.

The composer

Kevin Puts is best known for his operas, among them Silent Night, which won the Pulitzer Prize for Music. This bassoon concerto is a shared commission, so its premiere is part of a wider project that links three American orchestras.

What to listen for

The Price piece began as a piano suite, and Still's orchestration gives it a full orchestral sound before the premiere arrives. The bassoon is often heard in the background of an orchestra, so a world premiere that puts the instrument at the front of a major New York program is a rare opportunity. LeClair plays the solo line over the full orchestra, and the concerto is built to show the instrument's range from its lowest notes to its most singing lines.

Tickets

Tickets are sold through the New York Philharmonic's website. Each date is listed under the Wu Tsai Theater, so check the day and hall before you buy.
