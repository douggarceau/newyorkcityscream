Robert Eggers Is Bringing a Werewolf Home for Christmas

Focus Features drops the trailer for "Werwulf," a 13th-century horror film with Aaron Taylor-Johnson and Willem Dafoe, opening December 25.

Robert Eggers is bringing a werewolf to the multiplex on Christmas Day. Focus Features has released the official trailer for "Werwulf," the director's fifth feature, and it opens in theaters December 25, 2026, according to The Playlist.

What it is

The film is set around the year 1300, in 13th-century England and the forests of Wales, The Playlist reports. Focus pitches it as a harrowing tale of devotion, damnation and the devil within. Translation: this is not the family option for the holiday weekend.

Who is in it

Aaron Taylor-Johnson leads the cast, joined by Lily-Rose Depp, Willem Dafoe and Ralph Ineson, per The Playlist. If those names sound familiar, they should: Eggers keeps returning to the same company of actors, and several of them were in "Nosferatu." Eggers wrote the script with the Icelandic writer Sjón, his co-writer on "The Northman." Producers include Eggers, Sjón, Tim Bevan, Eric Fellner and Garrett Bird.

The Eggers track record

"Werwulf" follows "The Witch," "The Lighthouse," "The Northman" and "Nosferatu." His thing is period horror built on obsessive research, with folklore taken at face value. The Playlist notes that in a first-look interview with Esquire, Eggers talked about medieval werewolf lore and how wolves vanished from England.

What the people involved are saying

Eggers told Polygon the new film is more of an evolution than a leap from "Nosferatu," The Playlist reports. Focus chairman Peter Kujawski was less modest, telling Letterboxd that the film is on a whole other level. Focus first showed the project at CinemaCon earlier this year.

What comes next

The Christmas Day slot puts a medieval monster movie up against the season's big family releases, a classic counterprogramming play. Fans in Los Angeles already got an early taste: Beyond Fest held a Robert Eggers marathon at the Egyptian Theatre with a Q&A and exclusive footage. After this one, The Playlist says, Eggers is set to direct a "Romeo and Juliet" project for Focus, which won a bidding war for it.
