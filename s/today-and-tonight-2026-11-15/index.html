New York Today and Tonight: Tosca, Latin Soul and Holiday Trains

Sunday, November 15: the holiday train show and Autumn Bazaar open at 10 a.m., Dudamel's Tosca starts at 2 p.m. at Carnegie Hall, and Salcedo's Latin Soul plays at 3 p.m. in Flushing.

Sunday in New York can be as quiet or as full as you like. Start in the morning with the holiday train show in the Bronx or the Autumn Bazaar on the Upper West Side, then head to Midtown for Gustavo Dudamel's concert performance of Puccini's Tosca at Carnegie Hall, or to Flushing for Salcedo's Latin Soul.
