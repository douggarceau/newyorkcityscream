Holiday Season Arrives: The 100th Macy's Parade, the Tree and the Rockettes

The Rockefeller Center tree arrives Nov. 7, the Christmas Spectacular is underway at Radio City, and Macy's marches for the 100th time on Thanksgiving morning.

New York's holiday season gets rolling in November, and this year's Thanksgiving parade is a milestone.

Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade steps off at 8:30 a.m. on Thursday, Nov. 26, and runs until about noon. The route starts at Central Park West and 77th Street, heads south to Columbus Circle, turns east, then runs down Sixth Avenue to Macy's Herald Square. The parade dates to 1924. Arrive early for a curbside spot along the route; the giant balloons are inflated the day before.

The Rockefeller Center tree

The 2026 Rockefeller Center Christmas tree arrives Saturday, Nov. 7, on Meet the Tree Day. The lighting ceremony is Wednesday, Dec. 2; performers and hosts have not been announced. The Rink at Rockefeller Center is already open for the season, having opened Oct. 10.

The Rockettes

The Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes is back at Radio City Music Hall, 1260 Avenue of the Americas, with performances listed starting Wednesday, Nov. 4, and running through Jan. 4, 2027. Weekday shows generally start at 3 or 7 p.m., with weekend shows from 11 a.m. into the evening; check the specific date before buying.
