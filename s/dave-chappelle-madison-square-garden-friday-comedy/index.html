Dave Chappelle Brings Stand-Up to Madison Square Garden Friday

Friday, November 6 at 7:30 PM at Madison Square Garden, 4 Pennsylvania Plaza, as part of the New York Comedy Festival.

Dave Chappelle takes the stage at Madison Square Garden on Friday night, November 6, with a 7:30 PM start. The show is part of the New York Comedy Festival, the citywide celebration of stand-up comedy, and it puts one of the best-known names in American comedy in one of the city's largest arenas.

The arena tour

Chappelle's fall 2026 arena tour was announced in September as a limited run of large venues. Coverage at the time described the Madison Square Garden date as the tour's finale and tied it to the opening of the New York Comedy Festival.

Getting there

Madison Square Garden sits above Penn Station on Seventh Avenue between West 31st and West 33rd Streets. The 1, 2 and 3 trains, along with the A, C and E, stop at Penn Station. Doors and arrival times are set by the venue, so check the event listing before you leave home.

Tickets

Tickets are listed on StubHub and other ticket sites, and the venue listing is the place to confirm seat locations and entry rules. Anyone planning a full evening can pair the show with dinner in Midtown before the performance.
