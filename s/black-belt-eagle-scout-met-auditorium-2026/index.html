Black Belt Eagle Scout and Joe Rainey at the Met on November 17

Tuesday, November 17 at 7 PM in the Grace Rainey Rogers Auditorium at The Met Fifth Avenue, 1000 Fifth Avenue.

Black Belt Eagle Scout and Joe Rainey perform in the Grace Rainey Rogers Auditorium at The Met Fifth Avenue on Tuesday, November 17 at 7 PM. The auditorium is the museum's main concert and lecture space, a traditional theatre with a stage and a red curtain, built in 1954.

The room was designed for talks as well as concerts. Its walls are made of Korina wood, the acoustic design includes sound reflectors, and a sound lock at the entrance keeps outside noise out. Visitors reach the auditorium through gallery 135 in the Egyptian Wing.

The auditorium has hosted work across the performing arts. In 2014 it staged Ryoji Ikeda's superposition, which used twenty-one screens, and Gloria—A Pig Tale, which included a mud pit.

About the band

The band's latest record, as the Times Leader reported in 2023, was inspired by a return home to the ancestral lands of the Swinomish Tribe. The same report noted a July appearance at the Pitchfork Music Festival in Chicago.

Check the Met's website for ticket details and admission before you go, and allow time to find the auditorium's entrance in the Egyptian Wing.
