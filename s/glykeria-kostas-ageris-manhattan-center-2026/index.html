Glykeria and Kostas Ageris Sing in Manhattan on November 14

Saturday, November 14 at 9 PM in the Grand Ballroom at Manhattan Center in Midtown.

Glykeria and Kostas Ageris take the stage together in Manhattan on Saturday, November 14, at 9 PM in the Grand Ballroom at Manhattan Center. Greek-language press is reporting the evening as the launch of EXO NYC.

Who is singing

Glykeria is one of Greece's most celebrated singers, with a voice that has carried generations of Greek song from the stage into homes across the world. Kostas Ageris joins her on the bill for the evening.

Why it's worth the trip

For many New Yorkers of Greek heritage, a concert like this is a chance to hear Greek song performed live in Manhattan on a Saturday night. Manhattan Center sits in Midtown near Penn Station, and the listing carries the ticket link and details.
