LaGuardia Students Stage Godspell in Queens, Through November 22

Open dress rehearsal Wednesday, November 18 at 5 p.m.; performances through Sunday, November 22 at 2 p.m., LaGuardia Community College's Performing Arts Center, Long Island City, Queens.

Some of the most heartfelt theater in the city this week is happening on a community college campus. LaGuardia Community College's theater program presents Godspell, the 1971 musical, in a production staffed entirely by students, from the cast to the production team.

The show follows eight characters who are not from the Bible, alongside Jesus and John the Baptist, as they move through parables drawn mainly from the Gospel of Matthew. The staging uses games, storytelling and improv comedy, and the company is built around young performers learning to listen, trust and build relationships with one another.

Schedule

The open dress rehearsal is Wednesday, November 18 at 5 p.m., and it is at no charge. Performances follow on Thursday, November 19 and Friday, November 20 at 7 p.m., Saturday, November 21 at 5 p.m., and a Sunday, November 22 matinee at 2 p.m.

Getting in

General admission is $10 and student tickets are $8 for the four public performances. Purchase details are listed on the college's performing arts site, lpac.nyc.

Community theater shows like this one rarely get press coverage, which is exactly why they are worth the trip. An evening in the Little Theater is a chance to see young performers work with real craft, and to support a college arts program that gives students a stage of their own.
