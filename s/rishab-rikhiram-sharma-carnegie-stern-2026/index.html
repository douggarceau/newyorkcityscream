Sitarist Rishab Rikhiram Sharma Plays Carnegie Hall on November 21

Saturday, November 21 at Stern Auditorium in Carnegie Hall, Midtown.

Sitarist Rishab Rikhiram Sharma performs in Stern Auditorium at Carnegie Hall on Saturday, November 21. The concert brings the sitar, one of the great instruments of Indian classical music, to one of the most famous halls in the world.

A touring sitarist

Deccan Chronicle reports that Sharma made a sold-out debut at the Royal Albert Hall in London, and that he has announced an Asia tour beginning in Singapore. This New York date is part of a busy international schedule.

The hall

Stern Auditorium is the largest of Carnegie Hall's main spaces, at 881 Seventh Avenue. The 57th Street-Seventh Avenue station on the N, Q, R and W lines is a short walk away. Check the venue's calendar for the start time.
