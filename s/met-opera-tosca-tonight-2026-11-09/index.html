Puccini's Tosca Opens at the Met Tonight, Monday, November 9

Curtain is at 7:30 PM at the Metropolitan Opera House, 30 Lincoln Center Plaza, for one of Puccini's best-loved operas.

Giacomo Puccini's Tosca is on the Metropolitan Opera stage tonight, with curtain at 7:30 PM at the Metropolitan Opera House at Lincoln Center. The Upper West Side house is one of the busiest opera stages in the world, and Puccini's story of love, art and courage in Rome in 1800 is a natural choice for a Monday evening in November.

Tosca had its premiere in Rome in January 1900. The score is known for melodies that audiences hum on the way out of the theatre, and for the second-act aria Vissi d'arte, in which the title heroine sings of a life devoted to art and to love. The three acts move quickly through a church, a palace and a castle rooftop at dawn.

Tickets are sold through the Met's box office and resale sites. The cast and conductor are not listed on the ticket page used for this story, so check the Met's calendar before you go to see who is singing tonight.
