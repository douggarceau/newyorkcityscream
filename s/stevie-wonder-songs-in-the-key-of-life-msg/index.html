Stevie Wonder Brings 'Songs in the Key of Life' to the Garden

Stevie Wonder marks 50 years of his landmark double album with a Madison Square Garden show on Thursday, Nov. 19.

Stevie Wonder plays Madison Square Garden on Thursday, Nov. 19, on his Songs in the Key of Life 50th Anniversary Tour, celebrating half a century of the album.

Doors open at 7 p.m. and the show starts at 8 p.m. The Garden is at 4 Pennsylvania Plaza in Midtown Manhattan.

It is the marquee night of a heavy month at the Garden, which also hosts Dave Matthews Band on Nov. 12 and 13, Karan Aujla on Nov. 18 and SOMBR on Nov. 23 and 24.

Tickets are on sale now; check the Garden's official site for remaining seats and prices.
