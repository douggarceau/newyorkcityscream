Phish Takes Over Madison Square Garden for New Year's

Four nights from Dec. 30 to Jan. 2, with the second of January billed as the 100th Phish show at the Garden.

Phish is taking over Madison Square Garden for New Year's, and the whole run is built around one number. JamBase calls it the Road to 100. The Jan. 2 show is the 100th Phish concert at the Garden.

The run is four nights: Dec. 30, Dec. 31 for New Year's Eve, Jan. 1 and Jan. 2, 2027. The listings show a 7:30 p.m. start on each night. The Garden sits on Pennsylvania Plaza in Midtown, right across from Penn Station, so the train is the easy way in.

Tickets went on sale in October. According to JamBase, the first step was a presale lottery through Phish's ticketing, followed by hotel and ticket packages and a general on-sale that opened Oct. 16. The buy links ran through Ticketmaster. If you missed those windows, check Ticketmaster or the resale market for what is left.

What to know before you go

Doors and any opening act are not in the sources we checked, so look at the ticket page before you leave. Four nights is a lot of Garden. Pick the one you want most, or plan to catch the first night and see how the week feels.

JamBase also reports that hotel packages were sold alongside the tickets, which matters if you are coming in from out of town. Check the hotel side as well if you need a room.

The number lands on the second of January. Everything before it builds to that one show, and that is the reason a New Year's run at the Garden feels like an event rather than a booking.
