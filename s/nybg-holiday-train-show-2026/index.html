NYBG's Holiday Train Show Runs Through January 10 in the Bronx

Opens Saturday, November 14, with timed entry from 10 a.m., at the Enid A. Haupt Conservatory, New York Botanical Garden, Bronx.

Each winter the New York Botanical Garden sends model trains through a miniature city built from its own natural materials. This year's Holiday Train Show opens on Saturday, November 14 and runs through January 10, 2027.

The trains wind past about 200 miniature replicas of New York landmarks, from skyscrapers to bridges. The models are made from materials such as bark, leaves, pinecones and twigs, so the familiar skyline appears in a setting entirely of the garden's own making.

Plan the visit

Timed daytime entry begins at 10 a.m., with slots through 5:15 p.m. on applicable dates. On select evenings the Conservatory stays open until 8 p.m., with last entry at 7 p.m. Hours vary by date, so check the garden's official calendar before you go.

Getting there

The show is in the Enid A. Haupt Conservatory at 2900 Southern Boulevard in the Bronx. Ticket prices vary by date and ticket type, and the garden's official ticket calendar is the place to check.

A family outing

It makes a fine afternoon with children, grandparents or anyone who loves model railroads. The Conservatory's warm, green setting is a welcome change from the gray of late November.
