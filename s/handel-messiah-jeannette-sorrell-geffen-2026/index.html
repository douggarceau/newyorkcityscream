Jeannette Sorrell Conducts Handel's Messiah at David Geffen Hall

Four performances from Wednesday, Dec. 9 to Sunday, Dec. 13 in the Wu Tsai Theater at Lincoln Center, with Apollo's Singers and four soloists.

Handel's Messiah has been a December fixture in New York for generations, and this year Jeannette Sorrell leads it at David Geffen Hall. Sorrell conducts Apollo's Singers and four soloists in a presentation by Gary W. Parr, with four performances across five days in the Wu Tsai Theater.

Dates and times

The performances are on Wednesday, Dec. 9 and Thursday, Dec. 10 at 7 p.m., Saturday, Dec. 12 at 7 p.m. and Sunday, Dec. 13 at 2 p.m. The Wu Tsai Theater is inside David Geffen Hall, 10 Lincoln Center Plaza, on the Upper West Side.

The soloists

Jeanine De Bique, soprano, Rachael Wilson, mezzo-soprano, Ed Lyon, tenor, and Paweł Horodyski, bass, sing the solo parts. Apollo's Singers, which Sorrell leads as artistic director, provides the chorus.

About the work

Handel composed Messiah in 1741, and it premiered in Dublin in 1742. Its best-known movement, the Hallelujah chorus, is one of the most recognized pieces in the choral repertoire. The oratorio is at its most familiar at Christmas, when it has become a seasonal tradition for audiences and choirs alike.

The Oratorio Society describes its own performance as running about two and a half hours with an intermission, so plan your evening accordingly. Holiday audiences often find that a Messiah concert is a good first introduction to Handel's choral writing.

Another option

The Oratorio Society of New York presents its own Messiah at Carnegie Hall's Stern Auditorium on Monday, Dec. 21 at 7 p.m. The society says it has performed the work without interruption for 152 consecutive years, and single tickets are sold through Carnegie Hall's website.
