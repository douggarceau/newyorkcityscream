New York Results: Hochul Wins, All Five City Proposals Pass

The AP projected Gov. Kathy Hochul's re-election over Bruce Blakeman, and city voters approved all five charter questions.

Gov. Kathy Hochul won a second full term Tuesday. The Associated Press projected her win over Republican Bruce Blakeman at about 9:15 p.m., amNY reported. Her running mate is Adrienne Adams. Final, certified totals will come from the New York State Board of Elections.

Five city ballot questions

New York City voters approved all five charter proposals, according to unofficial city Board of Elections results reported by amNY. The proposals came from the city's Commission on Government Efficiency.
