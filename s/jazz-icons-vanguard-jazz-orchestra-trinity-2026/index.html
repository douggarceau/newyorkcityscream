The Vanguard Jazz Orchestra Brings Big-Band Jazz to Trinity Church

Sunday, Nov. 22 at 4 p.m. at Trinity Church on Broadway, part of the Jazz Icons series. The concert is sold out but can be watched online.

On Sunday, Nov. 22, the Vanguard Jazz Orchestra plays Trinity Church at 89 Broadway in Lower Manhattan as part of the church's Jazz Icons series. The concert starts at 4 p.m. and runs until about 5:15 p.m.

A big band in a historic church

Trinity's listing says this is the first time a big band has appeared in the Jazz Icons series. It describes the music as rousing big-band sound from true leaders in the tradition. The orchestra is closely associated with its long-running Monday residency at the Village Vanguard in Greenwich Village.

Watching from home

The concert is marked sold out on Trinity's listing. The church says viewers can still watch online by going to Trinity's homepage at the time of the concert.

The fall season

Trinity's fall 2026 season pairs American tradition with new work. Other events include the Concerts at One series and a Steve Reich series, including a Sandbox Percussion program of Reich's Drumming on Nov. 19 that is already on this site.

Trinity's music program has presented concerts throughout its fall season, and this big-band date adds a new sound to the Jazz Icons series. For listeners who know the orchestra from its Village Vanguard nights, the church setting offers a different way to hear the band's ensemble playing and arrangements.

Trinity Church sits at the head of Wall Street, a short walk from the Wall Street subway station, and it is one of the best-known landmarks in Lower Manhattan.
