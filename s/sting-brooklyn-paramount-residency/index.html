Sting Settles In for Eight Nights at Brooklyn Paramount

Sting brings his stripped-down STING 3.0 trio to Downtown Brooklyn for eight shows between Nov. 11 and Nov. 21.

Sting is taking up residence in Brooklyn this month. His STING 3.0 trio, with Dominic Miller and Chris Maas, plays Brooklyn Paramount on Nov. 11, 12, 14, 15, 17, 18, 20 and 21.

The format is stripped back: three players working through his solo catalog and songs from his years with The Police, in a room far smaller than the arenas he usually fills.

Brooklyn Paramount is at 385 Flatbush Avenue Extension in Downtown Brooklyn. Tickets are on sale through the venue's ticket links; with only eight nights, expect the weekend dates to go first.

The same stage closes the month with Tyla's A*Pop World Tour, on Nov. 28 and 29 at 8 p.m., bringing her mix of pop, R&B and amapiano.
