The New York Pops Brings Denée Benton and Jordan Donica to Carnegie Hall

Steven Reineke conducts One Night Only on Friday, November 20 at 8:00 PM at Carnegie Hall's Stern Auditorium in Midtown.

Steven Reineke leads The New York Pops at Carnegie Hall on Friday, November 20, at 8:00 PM, in the Stern Auditorium and Perelman Stage at 881 Seventh Avenue, at West 57th Street. The evening, titled One Night Only, brings two Tony-nominated performers to the stage: Denée Benton, who plays Peggy Scott in HBO's The Gilded Age, and Jordan Donica, an actor, singer, writer and director who plays Dr. William Kirkland in the same series.

The New York Pops has long been one of the city's most loved orchestras for the American songbook and Broadway. Its subscription series at Carnegie Hall is a fixture of the Midtown concert calendar, and this program carries the same spirit: strong voices, theatre songs and a full orchestra playing in one of the world's best-known halls.

Reineke, the orchestra's music director and conductor, has shaped the Pops' sound for years, mixing popular song with arrangements that give a symphony orchestra room to swing. Guest artists in an evening like this share the stage with the orchestra rather than simply taking a turn at the microphone, so the audience hears the singers in full theatrical form.

Tickets and planning

Tickets are sold through Carnegie Hall, and the ticket link on the Pops' own event page takes you there. The 57th Street subway stations are a short walk from the hall, so the evening is easy to reach from most parts of the city. Arrive a little early, since Carnegie's lobbies fill up before a Friday evening show.

It is a good way to hear the orchestra in its most theatrical mode, and a reminder that the Pops keeps the great American songbook alive in a hall built for the orchestral repertoire.
