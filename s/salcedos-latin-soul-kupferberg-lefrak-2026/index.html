Salcedo's Latin Soul Sunday Afternoon at Kupferberg Center in Flushing

Sunday, November 15 at 3 PM in LeFrak Concert Hall, Kupferberg Center for the Arts, 65-30 Kissena Boulevard, Flushing.

A Sunday afternoon concert is an easy plan, and Salcedo's Latin Soul gives Queens a warm one. The show takes place at 3 PM on Sunday, November 15 in LeFrak Concert Hall, one of the performance spaces at the Kupferberg Center for the Arts in Flushing.

The venue

The Kupferberg Center sits on the Queens College campus at 65-30 Kissena Boulevard, which makes it a destination for families, students and neighbors across northeast Queens. Allow a little extra time to find the right entrance on the campus.

The concert

Listings describe the show as a Latin concert and give no more detail about the program or the performers, so check the ticket page for any update to the lineup before you go.

A little background

Latin music has a deep history in New York, from the salsa and boogaloo dance floors of the 1960s and 1970s to the Queens neighborhoods where many of its musicians live and play today. A Sunday matinee is a relaxed way to hear that tradition close up.
