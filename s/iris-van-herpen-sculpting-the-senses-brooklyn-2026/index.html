Iris van Herpen: Sculpting the Senses at the Brooklyn Museum Through December 6

More than 140 haute couture works share the galleries at the Brooklyn Museum, 200 Eastern Parkway, in the designer's North American debut.

Iris van Herpen: Sculpting the Senses is on view at the Brooklyn Museum through December 6, 2026. It is the designer's North American debut, and the museum presents more than 140 haute couture creations alongside contemporary artworks, scientific artifacts and natural history specimens. The exhibition was organized by the Musée des Arts Décoratifs in Paris.

The idea behind the show

The exhibition looks at the body's place in space, its relationship to clothing and environment, and its future in a changing world. Van Herpen draws on biomimicry, fractal geometry and neuroscience, and the galleries move from the depths of the ocean to the outer reaches of the universe.

Sound and light

A soundscape by composer Salvador Breed adds a multisensory layer to the galleries. Visitors can explore the fluidity of water, the physics of motion and the way people perceive sound and light, with natural history pieces such as coral, fossils and skeletons placed among the garments.

Visiting

The Brooklyn Museum is at 200 Eastern Parkway in Prospect Heights. The museum's exhibition page does not list ticket prices or hours, so check the museum's website before you go.
