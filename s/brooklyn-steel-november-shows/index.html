Brooklyn Steel's November Weekends: Winyah and TroyBoi

The Brooklyn club hosts Winyah on Nov. 14 and producer TroyBoi on Nov. 28.

Brooklyn Steel, the 1,800-capacity room at 319 Frost Street in Brooklyn, has two Saturday nights worth circling.

Winyah headlines on Saturday, Nov. 14, with doors at 8 p.m. Two weeks later, on Saturday, Nov. 28, electronic producer TroyBoi takes over, with doors at 9 p.m.

Tickets for both are on sale through AXS and SeatGeek.
