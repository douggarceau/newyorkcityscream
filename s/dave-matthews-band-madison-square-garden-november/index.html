Dave Matthews Band Back at the Garden for Two Nights

Dave Matthews Band plays Madison Square Garden Nov. 12 and 13 on its Fall Tour 2026, with a different setlist each night.

Dave Matthews Band brings its Fall Tour 2026 to Madison Square Garden for two nights, Thursday, Nov. 12, and Friday, Nov. 13.

Expect the band's familiar blend of rock, jazz and folk, long jams and setlists that change from night to night, which makes the second show worth a look even if you caught the first.

The Garden is at 4 Pennsylvania Plaza in Midtown Manhattan. It is a busy stretch for the arena: Karan Aujla brings his P-Pop Culture World Tour there on Nov. 18 with special guest Ikky, Stevie Wonder follows on Nov. 19, and SOMBR plays Nov. 23 and 24 at 7 p.m. with Dove Cameron and Hannah Jadagu.

Tickets are on sale through the Garden's official ticket pages.
