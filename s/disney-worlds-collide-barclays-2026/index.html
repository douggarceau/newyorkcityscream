Disney Worlds Collide Brings Three Franchises to Barclays Center Tonight

Saturday, November 7 at 7 p.m. (doors at 6 p.m.), Barclays Center, 620 Atlantic Avenue, Prospect Heights, Brooklyn.

Tonight is a big one for families who love Disney's music. The Disney Worlds Collide Concert Tour brings the Descendants, ZOMBIES and Camp Rock franchises together in one arena show at Barclays Center in Brooklyn. The Saturday performance begins at 7 p.m., and doors open an hour earlier, at 6 p.m. This year Camp Rock joins the lineup for the first time.

The production blends hits from all three franchises with new mashups, choreography, special effects and plenty of audience participation. The cast includes Malachi Barton, Liamani Segura, Dara Reneé, Mekonnen Knife, Hudson Stone, Swayam Bhatia, Kiara Romero and Alexandro Byrd.

Before you go

Fans are encouraged to come in costume and sing along. An arena this size fills quickly, so an early arrival helps. Barclays Center sells tickets through Ticketmaster, and the venue's site also lists premium seating and group options. For group questions, the venue lists groups@bkse.com. The arena sits at 620 Atlantic Avenue, close to the Atlantic Avenue–Barclays Center subway hub.
