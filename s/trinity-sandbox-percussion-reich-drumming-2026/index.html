Sandbox Percussion Brings Steve Reich's Drumming to Trinity Church

Thursday, November 19 at 6:30 p.m. at Trinity Church, 89 Broadway, Lower Manhattan, with Sandbox Percussion and the students they have mentored.

On Thursday evening, Sandbox Percussion and the students it has mentored take the stage at Trinity Church for a performance of Steve Reich's Drumming. The concert is part of Trinity's Legacy of Steve Reich series, and it honors the teachers who shape the next generation of players.

Drumming is one of Reich's landmark minimalist works. Its patterns build slowly from small repeated figures, and the players must listen closely to one another to keep the music locked together.

A year for Reich

Steve Reich turns 90 this year, and programs like this one are part of a wider run of celebrations for the composer. Hearing a generation of percussionists tackle his music in a historic church makes the anniversary feel personal.

What to know

The performance begins at 6:30 p.m. Trinity Church is at 89 Broadway. Check the church's events pages for ticket details before you go.
