New York Today and Tonight: Dudamel's Tosca, Dave Matthews at MSG

Friday, November 13: Gustavo Dudamel leads Tosca at 7:30 p.m. at Carnegie Hall, and Dave Matthews Band plays Madison Square Garden tonight.

Friday brings one of the strongest evenings of the month for music lovers. Gustavo Dudamel and the New York Philharmonic open Carnegie Hall's opera-in-concert project tonight with Puccini's Tosca at 7:30 p.m. Dave Matthews Band is at Madison Square Garden for the second of its two nights. Earlier in the day, the Met's Morton Feldman program fills the Robert Lehman Wing from 3 p.m.
