Barclays Center's November: Young Miko to John Summit

Brooklyn's arena packs November with reggaeton, R&B, alt-rock and two nights of dance music.

Barclays Center, at 620 Atlantic Avenue in Brooklyn, runs a full slate of concerts this month.

Young Miko brings the Late Checkout Tour on Thursday, Nov. 5, with doors at 7 p.m. and the show at 8 p.m. Jessie Reyez follows on Thursday, Nov. 19, with her A Little Vengeance Tour, also doors at 7 and show at 8.

The Neighbourhood plays two nights on THE WOURLD TOUR, Saturday, Nov. 21, at 7:30 p.m. and Sunday, Nov. 22, at 6 p.m. Nessa Barrett opens both nights, with Noise Dept. added on Saturday only.

Thanksgiving weekend belongs to John Summit, whose CTRL ESCAPE Arena Tour lands Nov. 28 and 29 with Cassian and Jackie Hollander on both bills. Earlier in the month, Israeli pop star Omer Adam plays Nov. 1 and Nov. 3.

Tickets are sold through the arena's official ticket links.
