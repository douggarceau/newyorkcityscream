Gustavo Dudamel Leads the New York Philharmonic in Tosca at Carnegie Hall

Friday, November 13 at 7:30 p.m. and Sunday, November 15 at 2 p.m., Stern Auditorium at Carnegie Hall in Midtown Manhattan.

Gustavo Dudamel's first season as Music and Artistic Director of the New York Philharmonic opens with two concert performances of Puccini's Tosca at Carnegie Hall. The first is Friday, November 13 at 7:30 p.m., and the second is Sunday, November 15 at 2 p.m., both on the Stern Auditorium stage. The performances mark Dudamel's first appearances leading the Philharmonic at Carnegie Hall.

The cast is led by soprano Marina Rebeka in the title role, tenor Jonas Kaufmann as the painter Cavaradossi, and baritone Ludovic Tézier as Baron Scarpia. The New York Philharmonic Chorus, directed by Malcolm J. Merriweather, joins the orchestra. The three leads are returning to New York after several years away, and Rebeka and Tézier have recently performed together in Verdi's Nabucco and Simon Boccanegra in Naples.

A story set in Rome

Tosca is set in Rome in the summer of 1800, while French and Austrian forces contest the city. A painter, a singer and a powerful baron form the heart of the drama, and Puccini fills the score with some of opera's most loved melodies. Hearing the work in concert puts the focus on the singers and the orchestra, which is the point of the opera-in-concert format.

Tickets and the series

The Philharmonic and Carnegie Hall have formed a partnership to present opera in concert at the Hall each year, and this Tosca launches the series. Carnegie Hall's box office is at 57th Street and Seventh Avenue, and the team can be reached at 212-247-7800. Single tickets went on sale in August, so check carnegiehall.org for current availability. Dates, artists and prices are subject to change.
