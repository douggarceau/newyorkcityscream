Saint-Saëns's Samson et Dalila Comes to the Met on December 18

Friday, December 18 at 7 p.m. at the Metropolitan Opera House, Lincoln Center, Upper West Side; check metopera.org for casting.

The Metropolitan Opera's December season includes Camille Saint-Saëns's Samson et Dalila, one of the great works of the French repertory. The performance listed for December 18 begins at 7 p.m. at the Metropolitan Opera House at Lincoln Center.

The opera

Saint-Saëns drew his story from the Book of Judges and set it in French, with a score that moves between sweeping choruses and intimate duets. The opera premiered in Weimar in 1877, and it has remained one of the composer's most performed stage works.

Its best-known number is Dalila's aria, Mon cœur s'ouvre à ta voix, which many listeners know from recordings and concert programs long before they see the opera in full.

The December season

The Met's December list also includes La Bohème, a holiday presentation of The Magic Flute and Maria Stuarda, so the house offers a wide range of repertory in the weeks before the new year.

Planning the evening

Listings for the performance are available through ticket sites, with prices that vary by seat. Final casting, conducting details and production notes are best confirmed at metopera.org before you buy, since listing pages do not always include them.
