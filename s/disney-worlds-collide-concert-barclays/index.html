Disney's 'Worlds Collide' Tour Brings Family Sing-Alongs to Brooklyn

Songs from Descendants, ZOMBIES and Camp Rock fill Barclays Center on Saturday, Nov. 7.

Families with young fans should mark Saturday, Nov. 7, when the Disney Worlds Collide Concert Tour plays Barclays Center, 620 Atlantic Avenue in Brooklyn.

The show draws on songs from Descendants, ZOMBIES and Camp Rock, with performers including Malachi Barton, Liamani Segura and Dara Reneé.

Doors open at 6 p.m. and the show starts at 7 p.m., an early curtain that works for school-age kids. Tickets are on sale through the arena's official ticket links.
