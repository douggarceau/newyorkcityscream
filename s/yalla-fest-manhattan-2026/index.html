Yalla Fest Brings Lebanese Food, Art and Music to Broadway

On Saturday, November 21 from noon to 7 p.m., the festival fills the second floor at 837 Broadway with food, art, music and shopping.

Yalla Fest brings a day of Lebanese food, art, music and shopping to 837 Broadway in Manhattan on Saturday, November 21. The festival runs from noon to 7 p.m. on the second floor of the building, and the listing gives an entry charge of $25.

What to expect

The festival gathers several parts of a Levantine celebration in one place: food, visual art, live music and a marketplace of goods. The word yalla, which means let's go in Arabic, is a fitting invitation for a Saturday afternoon out.

Why it is worth the trip

Festivals of this kind are one of the easiest ways to meet a community's food and culture without leaving Manhattan. Visitors can taste, look, listen and shop in one afternoon, and a festival with live music and art brings out people from across the city.

Practical details

The festival runs from noon to 7 p.m. on November 21 at 837 Broadway, second floor. The listing gives an entry charge of $25, and it is a good choice for a relaxed afternoon in the neighborhood.
