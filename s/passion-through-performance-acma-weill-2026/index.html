Passion Through Performance: ACMA's Showcase Fills Weill Recital Hall

The annual ACMA concert runs Saturday, November 21 at 8 p.m. at Weill Recital Hall at Carnegie Hall, with solo and chamber works by many featured performers.

Passion Through Performance is the annual concert from ACMA, a showcase of solo and chamber music that fills Weill Recital Hall at Carnegie Hall with many featured performers. The concert takes place on Saturday, November 21 at 8 p.m. at Weill Recital Hall, 154 West 57th Street.

The program

The listing shows a varied evening. Early items include the third movement of Mozart's Quintet K. 452 and two songs from Wagner's Wesendonk Lieder. Later pieces include Dvořák's Slavonic Dance in E minor, Op. 72 No. 2, Rachmaninoff's Étude-Tableau in A minor, Op. 39 No. 2, and the second movement of Bach's Sonata No. 1 in G minor, BWV 1001, for solo violin.

The lineup also features Margin Alexander's The Silent Concerto, performed by a string ensemble with the composer at the piano, an excerpt from Kálmán's Die Csárdásfürstin, and Jonathan Gilbert's Ballade No. 1 for alto saxophone and piano. The program also includes Mozart's Fantasia in C minor, K. 475, and a group of Schubert songs, and the concert has an intermission.

Tickets and details

The listing points to a Meetup page and to Carnegie Hall's own website for tickets and details, and it does not state prices. It was last updated on November 13 and notes that times, lineup and availability can change, so confirm before you go.

For a concert that gathers many performers into one evening, it is a fine way to hear a wide range of repertoire in a recital hall.
