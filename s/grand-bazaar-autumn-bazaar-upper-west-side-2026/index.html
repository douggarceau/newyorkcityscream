Grand Bazaar's Autumn Bazaar Brings Makers and Vintage Sunday

Sunday, November 15 from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. at 100 West 77th Street at Columbus Avenue, Upper West Side.

Every Sunday, the Upper West Side has a bazaar that feels more like a neighborhood gathering than a shopping destination. On November 15, Grand Bazaar NYC holds its Autumn Bazaar at 100 West 77th Street, at Columbus Avenue.

The day brings together vintage collectors, local makers, artists, small businesses and food vendors. The organizers describe it as a day for neighborhood connection, and browsing is the main activity, so it is a pleasant way to spend a November morning.

Why it matters

The bazaar runs every Sunday all year, indoors and outdoors, and its organizers say it supports more than 2,000 local public school children. A fall gathering like this one keeps that community spirit going through the season.

Plan your visit

Hours are 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Check the bazaar's website for the current vendor list before you go, and bring a tote bag for the things you find.
