Paul Taylor Dance Company Brings Three Premieres to Lincoln Center

Runs November 3 to 22 at the David H. Koch Theater, Lincoln Center, Upper West Side, with showtimes listed at boxoffice.dance.

Paul Taylor Dance Company is back at the David H. Koch Theater for its annual New York season, running through November 22 at Lincoln Center. The three-week run mixes new work with the company's own repertory, and it gives the Upper West Side one of the most dependable dance events of the fall.

The season brings three world premieres. Lauren Lovette's 30 Incoming is set to music by The Books, while Robert Battle's A Starling pairs with John Mackey's Bells on the Garment of Silence. Pam Tanowitz contributes Messengers, Maidens and the End of Something Else, a New York premiere set to Schubert's Death and the Maiden.

Returning works and company premieres

Battle's Under the Rhythm, from 2025, returns to the program, and his No Longer Silent, a 2007 work, arrives as a Company premiere. Alwin Nikolais's Tensile Involvement, dating from 1953, also comes to the company for the first time, a rare chance to see a midcentury piece on a repertory stage.

Taylor's own works fill out the bill, including Esplanade, Brandenburgs, Arabesque, ...Byzantium, Spindrift, 3 Epitaphs, Airs, Cloven Kingdom, Last Look and Piazzolla Caldera. Orchestra of St. Luke's performs the music live, which gives every evening the feeling of a true concert as well as a dance program.

Getting tickets

Tickets are sold through boxoffice.dance, with prices starting at $19. The published listing does not include showtimes, so check the box office for the schedule before you plan the evening. The company's three-week run closes on November 22, which leaves little time to waste.

Paul Taylor's company has long been one of the most admired modern dance troupes in the country, and a November visit to the Koch Theater is a fine way to spend an evening at Lincoln Center.
