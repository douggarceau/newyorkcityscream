Biltmore Bazaar Brings Vintage and Makers to Grand Central's Track 42

Tuesday, November 17 from 8 a.m. in Grand Central Terminal's Track 42, with independent New York makers and vintage sellers.

Grand Central Terminal's Track 42 turns into a small bazaar on Tuesday, November 17, when Biltmore Bazaar sets up its vintage and makers market for the fall season. The fair starts at 8 a.m. at 89 East 42nd Street, in one of the city's most recognizable buildings.

The organizers describe it as a showcase for independent New York makers and vintage sellers, with one-of-a-kind fall finds. Vendors bring handmade art, clothing, metalsmith jewelry and accessories, so it is a good stop for holiday gifts that were made by hand rather than mass-produced.

Why it is worth the trip

Markets like this one give small studios and collectors a place to meet shoppers in a historic setting. The terminal makes an unusual backdrop for a fair, and a morning visit lets you browse at an easy pace before the day gets busy.

Plan your visit

The fair's page does not list hours beyond the 8 a.m. start, and admission details are not stated there, so check the ticket or entry listing linked from the fair's page before you go. Grand Central sits on 42nd Street between Park and Lexington avenues.
