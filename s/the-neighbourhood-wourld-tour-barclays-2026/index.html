The Neighbourhood Brings THE WOURLD TOUR to Barclays Center

Saturday, November 21 at 7:30 p.m. and Sunday, November 22 at 6 p.m. at Barclays Center in Downtown Brooklyn, with Nessa Barrett both nights.

This weekend, the alternative rock and pop band The Neighbourhood brings THE WOURLD TOUR to Barclays Center in Downtown Brooklyn for two nights. The Saturday show, November 21, begins at 7:30 p.m., and the Sunday show, November 22, begins at 6 p.m.

Nessa Barrett joins both nights, and Noise Dept. opens the Saturday show. The tour blends the band's moody guitar-driven songs with the pop hooks that have carried its records to large audiences.

Why it is a big weekend

Barclays Center is one of the largest indoor venues in New York, so a two-night arena run gives fans from across the region a chance to see a full-scale production in one place. Few concerts this fall offer a bigger stage for a band of this kind.

Getting there

Barclays Center sits on Atlantic Avenue in Downtown Brooklyn and is served by many subway lines. Tickets for both nights are sold through the tour's official listing, linked below.
