Raúl Esparza Stars in Galileo, a New Broadway Musical at the Shubert Theatre

Previews begin Tuesday, Nov. 10, and the show opens Sunday, Dec. 6, at the Shubert Theatre in the Theater District.

A new Broadway musical about Galileo Galilei begins previews at the Shubert Theatre on Tuesday, Nov. 10, with opening night set for Sunday, Dec. 6. The show follows an independent-minded scientist whose astronomical discoveries changed how people understood the universe, and it asks what happens when he must defend those findings against the most powerful religious institution of his time.

The creative team

Danny Strong wrote the book, and Zoe Sarnak and Michael Weiner wrote the music and lyrics. Michael Mayer directs, David Neumann choreographs, and the producers are Amanda Lipitz, Henry Tisch and Jordan Roth. Raúl Esparza plays the title role alongside Jeremy Kushnier and Joy Woods, and additional casting is still to be announced.

From Berkeley to Broadway

The musical premiered at Berkeley Repertory Theatre in California before its move to New York. It is one of the new musicals on the fall calendar, and the Shubert, on West 44th Street, has long been home to big musical productions.

Before you go

Dates, prices and seating change as a production moves through previews, so check the show's official listing for current information before you buy.

A story told many times

Galileo's clash with authority has been told on stage and screen many times, and Bertolt Brecht's play Life of Galileo is among the best-known versions. A musical version brings the same questions about science, faith and independent thought to a Broadway audience, with a score by Zoe Sarnak and Michael Weiner.
