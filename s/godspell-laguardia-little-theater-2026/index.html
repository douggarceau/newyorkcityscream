LaGuardia Students Stage Godspell at the Little Theater in Long Island City

LaGuardia Community College's theatre program presents Godspell, November 18 through 22, at the Little Theater in Long Island City.

The cast and production team are all LaGuardia students, working under the direction and choreography of Theater Program Director Stefanie Sertich.

Performances run Wednesday, November 18 at 5:00 PM (an open dress rehearsal with no charge), Thursday, November 19 at 7:00 PM, Friday, November 20 at 7:00 PM, Saturday, November 21 at 5:00 PM, and Sunday, November 22 at 2:00 PM. Tickets for the other four performances are $10 general admission, with student tickets starting at $8, and details are on the center's website, lpac.nyc.

About the show

Godspell is a two-act musical with a book by John-Michael Tebelak and lyrics by Stephen Schwartz. It debuted in 1971 and is built around parables drawn mainly from the Gospel of Matthew, but its real subject is community: a group of people finding one another through simple songs and play. Tebelak first developed the piece as a master's thesis project at Carnegie Mellon University before it moved to the experimental company La MaMa in New York, and Schwartz, also a Carnegie Mellon graduate, joined to write the score.

Why go

The set takes its inspiration from the observation towers of the New York State Pavilion in Flushing Meadows–Corona Park, a fitting nod to the borough where the college sits. Sertich has said the production aims to bring unity to the community, and it gives students hands-on work in acting, stagecraft and collaboration. The Wednesday open dress rehearsal is an easy way to see a show that is still taking shape.

The Little Theater is on the LaGuardia campus at 31-10 Thomson Avenue, a short walk from the Queensboro Plaza subway station. Arrive a little early to find your seat.
